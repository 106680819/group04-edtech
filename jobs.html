Development · ED101

Web Developer

Build clear, responsive learning pages that help students access lessons and teachers share useful resources.

Reference
ED101
Employment
Full time · Melbourne / Hybrid
Annual salary
AUD 80,000–95,000
Reports to
Development Lead

What you will do

  • Build and maintain accessible pages for our learning platform.
  • Turn design layouts into responsive HTML and CSS.
  • Test forms and navigation across different devices.
  • Work with designers and educators to improve the learning experience.

Essential requirements

  • Knowledge of semantic HTML, CSS and responsive layouts.
  • Understanding of keyboard access, readable content and labelled forms.
  • Ability to explain ideas clearly and work as part of a team.
  • Careful problem-solving and a willingness to learn.

Preferable skills

  • Experience using Git to track and share changes.
  • Previous coursework or projects involving learning platforms.

Design · ED102

UI/UX Designer

Design simple, welcoming digital experiences that help students and teachers find what they need with confidence.

Reference
ED102
Employment
Full time · Melbourne / Hybrid
Annual salary
AUD 75,000–90,000
Reports to
Design Lead

What you will do

  • Create wireframes and page layouts for learning tools.
  • Improve navigation, readability and the steps in everyday tasks.
  • Listen to feedback from learners and teachers.
  • Work with developers to turn designs into usable pages.

Essential requirements

  • Understanding of layout, typography and visual hierarchy.
  • Awareness of accessible and inclusive interface design.
  • A portfolio of design work, including coursework or personal projects.
  • Strong communication, teamwork and problem-solving skills.

Preferable skills

  • Experience making wireframes or prototypes in a design tool.
  • An interest in user research and educational technology.
